How I work

Four principles, each tested on a live platform.

Design for behaviour, not idealised efficiency

Adoption depends on respecting how experts already work. Interdealer swap traders trusted voice broking, so RTX kept negotiation private and bilateral with a Request-for-Trade model.

Seen in Interdealer swap trading

Organise around tasks, not data

Expert users act on tasks. STAN and TAM were restructured around the workflows traders and middle-office teams actually run, with analytics and reference data surfaced when they become relevant.

Seen in STAN TAM

Treat friction as an operational cost

A failed login or a manual reconciliation costs calls, errors and time. On HSBC's authentication platform, push and QR login reduced reliance on fragile OTP flows without weakening security.

Seen in Global web authentication

Lower cognitive load when stakes are high

Under pressure, restraint helps more than extra highlighting. Linking video, sensor data and comms in one spatial workspace let security operators decide faster with fewer false positives.

Seen in Command and control
